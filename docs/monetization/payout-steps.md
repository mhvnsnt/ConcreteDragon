# Concrete Dragon — itch.io payout setup (for the owner)

Price: **$4.99**. Payout research done 2026-10-06 from itch.io's own docs.

## The short answer
itch.io does **not** pay direct ACH to Cash App (or any bank) — payouts go through
**PayPal or Payoneer only**. So the money path is:

**itch.io → PayPal → your Cash App**

PayPal can send to any US bank account via ACH, and your Cash App account has
real routing + account numbers (it accepts ACH deposits). So: PayPal balance →
"transfer to bank" → enter Cash App routing/account numbers → lands in Cash App.

## Two ways to get paid on itch.io

### Option A — "Collected by itch.io, paid later" (recommended)
- Buyers pay itch.io; money pools in your itch.io account.
- You request a payout whenever you want (dashboard → Payouts).
- Payout goes to your connected **PayPal**.
- Requires: itch.io **tax interview** (your real info — US person, takes a few minutes).
- itch.io takes its cut per sale (you set the revenue share, default 10%), PayPal takes its fee on payout.

### Option B — "Direct to you"
- Each buyer pays **directly into your PayPal** at purchase time. Instant, no payout wait.
- You are the merchant of record (you handle refunds/tax forms via PayPal).
- Requires: PayPal connected on itch.io's end.

Either way you need a **PayPal account in your name** — that's the one account
I can't create for you (identity/KYC). Everything else (itch.io account, game
page, uploads) I handle.

## Your steps (about 10 minutes total)
1. Create/log into PayPal (paypal.com) — personal is fine to start.
2. In Cash App: Banking tab → get your **routing number** and **account number**.
3. In PayPal: link a bank → enter the Cash App routing + account numbers.
4. On itch.io (once the game page is up): complete the **tax interview**,
   then Dashboard → Payouts → connect PayPal.
5. When money comes in: itch.io payout → PayPal → transfer to bank (Cash App numbers).
